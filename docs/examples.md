# Things to ask

Talk to your AI app in your own words and language. These are ideas; the AI picks the tools.

## See what's happening

- *"What got blocked today? Show it as a chart."*
- *"Top 20 blocked domains this week on the Kids profile."*
- *"Which device makes the most queries?"*
- *"How much of my traffic is encrypted? And DNSSEC?"*
- *"Which countries and big tech companies does my traffic go to?"*
- *"Show the log for my iPhone in the last hour, only blocked."*
- *"Give me a link to download the whole log."*

## Find out why

- *"Why doesn't example.com load?"* (`check_domain`: a real query, plus the lists and the log)
- *"I'm opening the app now. Watch for 30 seconds and tell me what's blocked."* (`watch_logs`)
- *"Is tiktok.com blocked on the Kids profile, and by what?"*

## Lists

- *"Unblock example.com."*
- *"Block these: ads.example.com, tracker.example.net, telemetry.example.org."*
- *"Turn off the denylist entry for example.com for now, but keep it."*
- *"What's in my allowlist?"*

## Blocklists and protection

- *"Which blocklists do I have? What else is there from HaGeZi?"*
- *"Add OISD and remove everything except NextDNS Recommended."*
- *"Turn on Samsung and Xiaomi tracker protection."*
- *"Turn on AI threat detection and block newly registered domains."*
- *"Block the .zip and .mov TLDs."*

## Parental control

- *"Block TikTok, Instagram and Fortnite on the Kids profile."*
- *"Block gambling and dating."*
- *"Allow TikTok on Friday and Saturday from 6 to 9 pm, Lisbon time."*
- *"Turn on safe search and YouTube restricted mode."*
- *"Pause the Roblox block this afternoon."* (and later: *"resume it"*)

## Settings

- *"Keep logs for one week, stored in Switzerland."*
- *"Don't keep client IPs in the logs."*
- *"Turn on the block page and cache boost."*
- *"Rename this profile to Home."*
- *"Point nas.home to 192.168.1.10."* (a rewrite)

## Profiles

- *"Make a profile for my guest Wi-Fi, a copy of Home but without the rewrites."*
- *"What's different between Home and Kids?"*
- *"Give me the DNS-over-HTTPS address of the Kids profile for my router."*

## Undo and backups

- *"What did you change today?"*
- *"Undo the last change."*
- *"Back up the Kids profile before we change anything."*
- *"Put Home back the way it was in yesterday's backup."* (asks you twice)

## Careful ones (always asked twice)

- *"Delete the Test profile."*
- *"Clear the logs of the Guest profile."*

The AI must tell you exactly what will happen and get your yes before it can do these. See [safety.md](safety.md#asked-twice).
